📖 MINI LESSON

Meet the Eight Parts of Speech

Use these quick clues before entering the games.
01

Noun

Names a person, place, thing, or idea.

teacher • school • book • courage
02

Pronoun

Takes the place of a noun.

he • she • it • they
03

Verb

Shows an action or state of being.

run • think • is • become
04

Adjective

Describes a noun or pronoun.

bright • three • happy
05

Adverb

Describes a verb, adjective, or another adverb.

quickly • very • carefully
06

Preposition

Shows a relationship such as place, time, or direction.

under • beside • after
07

Conjunction

Connects words, phrases, or clauses.

and • but • because
08

Interjection

Expresses a sudden feeling or reaction.

Wow! • Ouch! • Hurray!

ⓘ ABOUT THE INTERVENTION

Learning Can Be Fun, Meaningful, and Encouraging.

This learning space is designed to give learners another opportunity to practice, understand, and grow.
♥

Why Intervention?

Intervention provides guided practice for learners who need more time, repetition, examples, and encouragement to strengthen a skill.

🎮

Learning Through Games

Interactive challenges turn practice into an active experience with hints, feedback, rewards, streaks, and short achievable goals.

📈

Progressive Levels

Easy builds confidence, Average strengthens skills, Difficult develops deeper thinking, and Master checks overall understanding.

🌱

Positive Learning Space

Mistakes are part of learning. The goal is steady improvement, stronger understanding, and confidence—not simply getting every answer right the first time.
