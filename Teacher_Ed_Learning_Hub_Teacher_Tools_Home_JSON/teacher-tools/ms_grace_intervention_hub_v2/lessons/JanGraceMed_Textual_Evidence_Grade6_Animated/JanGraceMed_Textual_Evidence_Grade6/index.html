What Is Textual Evidence?

Textual evidence is a specific detail, fact, action, quotation, or example from a text that supports an idea or answer.

🕵️

Think Like a Detective

Do not guess from memory. Return to the passage and look for proof that is directly connected to the claim.

CLAIM→EVIDENCE→EXPLANATION
01

Make a Claim

A claim is the idea or answer you think the text supports.

Maya is responsible.
02

Find Evidence

Choose a detail from the passage that gives direct proof.

Maya checked the class plants every morning.
03

Explain the Connection

Tell how the evidence proves or supports the claim.

Checking the plants shows that Maya takes responsibility.
💡 QUICK TIPAsk: “Where in the text can I prove my answer?”

Strong evidence is relevant, specific, and directly connected to the claim.
