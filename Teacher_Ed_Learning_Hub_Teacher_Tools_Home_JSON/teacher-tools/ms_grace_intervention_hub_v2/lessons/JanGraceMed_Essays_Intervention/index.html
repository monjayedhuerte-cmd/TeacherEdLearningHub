📖 MINI LESSON

Build an Essay Step by Step

Use this guide before entering the games.
01

Choose & Focus

Understand the topic and decide the main idea or position you want your essay to communicate.

02

Introduction

Introduce the topic, give useful background, and lead the reader toward the main idea or thesis.

03

Body Paragraphs

Develop your ideas with clear reasons, details, examples, facts, or other supporting evidence.

04

Transitions

Use connecting words and phrases to help readers follow how your ideas relate to one another.

05

Conclusion

Bring the main idea together, reinforce the important point, and give the essay a clear ending.

06

Revise & Edit

Check organization, clarity, supporting details, word choice, grammar, spelling, and punctuation.

💡 ESSAY MEMORY TIP: Plan → Introduce → Develop → Connect → Conclude → Revise

ⓘ ABOUT THE INTERVENTION

Learning Can Be Fun, Meaningful, and Encouraging.

Why Intervention?

Intervention provides additional guided practice, repetition, examples, and encouragement so learners can strengthen skills at a manageable pace.

🎮

Learning Through Games

Short challenges turn practice into an active experience with hints, feedback, rewards, streaks, and achievable goals.

📈

Progressive Levels

Easy builds confidence, Average strengthens skills, Difficult develops deeper thinking, and Master checks overall understanding.

🌱

A Positive Learning Space

Mistakes are part of learning. Learners are encouraged to try again, understand the explanation, and keep improving.
