📖 MINI LESSON

Build a Strong Sentence

Use these quick guides before entering the games.
01

Complete Thought

A complete sentence expresses a complete thought and normally contains a subject and a predicate.

The students practiced.
02

Subject

The subject tells who or what the sentence is about.

The children laughed.
03

Predicate

The predicate tells what the subject does or what the subject is.

The children laughed loudly.
04

Sentence Types

Statements, questions, commands, and exclamations have different purposes.

She reads. / Does she read?
05

Simple Sentence

Has one independent clause and expresses one main idea.

Maria studies.
06

Compound Sentence

Joins independent clauses, often with a coordinating conjunction such as and, but, or so.

I studied, and I practiced.
07

Fragment

A fragment is missing a necessary part or does not express a complete thought.

Because the bell rang.
08

Run-on Sentence

A run-on incorrectly joins complete thoughts without the needed punctuation or connection.

I ran home I was tired.
