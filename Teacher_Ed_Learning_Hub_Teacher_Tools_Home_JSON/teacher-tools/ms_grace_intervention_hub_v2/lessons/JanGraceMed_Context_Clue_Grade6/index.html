What Are Context Clues?

Context clues are words, phrases, sentences, or details around an unfamiliar word that help readers figure out what the word means.

🔎

Think Like a Detective

Do not stop when you see a difficult word. Read the whole sentence and nearby sentences. Look for clues that explain, describe, compare, contrast, or show an example.

UNKNOWN WORD+SURROUNDING CLUES→MEANING
01

Definition Clue

The meaning is stated directly.

Arid, or very dry, regions receive little rain.
02

Example Clue

Examples help explain the word.

Celestial objects, such as the sun, moon, and stars, fill the sky.
03

Contrast Clue

Opposite ideas can reveal meaning.

Unlike his timid brother, Marco was bold and fearless.
04

Synonym / Restatement

A nearby word or phrase gives a similar meaning.

The puppy was enormous—in other words, very large.
💡 QUICK TIPAsk: “What words around the unfamiliar word give me a clue?”Try the clue first, then choose the meaning that best fits the context.
