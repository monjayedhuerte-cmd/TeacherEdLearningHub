📖 MINI LESSON

Four Types of Sentences

Learn the purpose, punctuation, and examples before playing.
📘
01

Declarative

A declarative sentence makes a statement or gives information. It usually ends with a period.

The class starts at eight.
❓
02

Interrogative

An interrogative sentence asks a question. It ends with a question mark.

When does class start?
👉
03

Imperative

An imperative sentence gives a command, direction, request, or instruction.

Please open your book.
❗
04

Exclamatory

An exclamatory sentence expresses strong feeling or excitement and ends with an exclamation mark.

That was amazing!
💡 MEMORY TIP: Statement = tells • Question = asks • Command = tells someone to do something • Exclamation = shows strong feeling

ⓘ ABOUT THE INTERVENTION

Learning Can Be Fun, Meaningful, and Encouraging.

This intervention space gives learners another opportunity to practice, understand, and grow.
♥

Why Intervention?

Intervention gives learners additional guided practice, repetition, examples, and encouragement so they can strengthen skills at a manageable pace.

🎮

Learning Through Games

Short interactive challenges make practice active and engaging through hints, immediate explanations, rewards, streaks, and achievable goals.

📈

Progressive Levels

Easy builds confidence, Average strengthens skills, Difficult develops deeper thinking, and Master checks overall understanding.

🌱

A Positive Learning Space

Mistakes are part of learning. The goal is steady improvement, stronger understanding, and confidence—not simply getting every answer right immediately.
