Types of Figurative Language

Explore five ways writers make language vivid: simile, metaphor, personification, hyperbole, and idiom.

SimileMetaphorPersonificationHyperboleIdiom

Quick Guide

Simile

Compares using like or as.

Her smile is like sunshine.
Metaphor

Directly compares one thing to another.

The classroom was a zoo.
Personification

Gives human qualities to non-human things.

The wind whispered.
Hyperbole

Uses an obvious exaggeration.

I told you a million times.
Idiom

Has a nonliteral intended meaning.

Break a leg!
