📖 MINI LESSON

Types of Figurative Language

Learn the clues before you play.

01

Simile

Compares two things using like or as.

"Her smile was like sunshine."
02

Metaphor

Directly compares two things without like or as.

"The classroom was a zoo."
03

Personification

Gives human actions or qualities to non-human things.

"The wind whispered."
04

Hyperbole

Uses extreme exaggeration for emphasis or effect.

"I have told you a million times."
05

Idiom

A phrase whose meaning differs from its literal words.

"Break a leg!"
