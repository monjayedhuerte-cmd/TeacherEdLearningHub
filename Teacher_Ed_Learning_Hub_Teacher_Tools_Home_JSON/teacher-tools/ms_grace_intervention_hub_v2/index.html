ABOUT THE LEARNING HUB

Learning Can Be Fun, Meaningful, and Encouraging.

Welcome to Ms. Grace Intervention Learning Hub, an interactive learning space created to help learners practice essential Math and English skills through engaging lessons, activities, and educational games.

Our goal is to make intervention learning less stressful and more enjoyable by giving learners opportunities to practice, make mistakes, receive feedback, and try again.

OUR PURPOSE

Why This Learning Hub Exists

The Ms. Grace Intervention Learning Hub is designed to provide additional learning opportunities for learners who need more practice, reinforcement, or support in specific skills.

Intervention does not have to feel like a difficult or stressful experience. Through interactive activities and educational games, learners can practice important skills while enjoying the learning process.

The purpose is not simply to get the correct answer. The greater goal is to help learners understand, practice, improve, and become more confident.

Skill Reinforcement Strengthen important skills through repeated practice.
Additional Practice Provide learners with more opportunities to learn.
Interactive Learning Make practice more engaging through activities and games.
Build Confidence Encourage learners to believe in their ability to improve.
LEARNING THROUGH PLAY

A POSITIVE LEARNING SPACE

Mistakes Are Part of Learning.

Getting an answer wrong does not mean that a learner has failed. It means there is another opportunity to learn.

The Learning Hub encourages learners to review feedback, understand their mistakes, use hints when available, and try again.

Try again. Learn from it. Keep going.
