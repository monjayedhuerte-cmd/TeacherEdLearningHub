EXPLORE & LEARN

Meet the Five Senses

Your body has special parts that help you collect information. Scientists use observation to learn about the world.

👀

SEE

We use our eyes to see colors, people, shapes, and things.

👂

HEAR

We use our ears to hear music, voices, and sounds.

👃

SMELL

We use our nose to smell flowers, food, and perfume.

👅

TASTE

We use our mouth/tongue to taste food that may be sweet, sour, salty, or bitter.

✋

TOUCH

We use our fingers/skin to feel things that are soft, rough, hot, or cold.

🧠 Scientist's Idea

Observe means to look closely or use our senses to notice information. In science, we observe, investigate, gather information, work with others, and explain our ideas.

ACTIVITY 4

Read Like a Young Scientist

The World Around Us

In science, we observe the world around us. We use our senses to gather information. We may use our eyes, ears, nose, mouth, and fingers. Scientists also investigate, work with other people, and explain their ideas.

Think & Answer
