🎵 What Is a Rhyme?

Listen to the ending sounds.

🐱CAT
↔
🎩HAT

Cat and hat rhyme because they end with the sound /at/.

☀️ Pair 1

sun — fun

Both end with /un/.

⚽ Pair 2

ball — tall

Both end with /all/.

🍰 Pair 3

cake — snake

Both end with /ake/.

📖 What Does It Mean to Read?

1️⃣ Look at words.
2️⃣ Notice letters and sounds.
3️⃣ Think about meaning.
4️⃣ Share what you understand.

💡 Main Idea Example

Text: “Books can teach us many things. We can learn about animals, places, people, and new ideas by reading books.”

Main idea: Books help us learn many things.
