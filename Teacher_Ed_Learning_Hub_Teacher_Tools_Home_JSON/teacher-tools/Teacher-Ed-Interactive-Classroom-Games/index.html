TEACHER SUPPORT

💡 Motivation Teaching Tips

Use the game as a bridge to learning—not as entertainment alone.

1. ATTENTION

Start with: “Look at this!” or “I have a challenge for you.”

2. PARTICIPATION

Give students a clear response method: raise a hand, vote, move, discuss, or answer.

3. THINKING

Ask students to explain why they chose an answer—not only whether it is correct.

4. DISCUSSION

Invite different answers and let students compare their ideas.

5. CONNECTION

Connect their responses directly to the learning target or topic.

6. TRANSITION

Finish with: “Now let’s discover why…” and begin the lesson.

🎤
Universal transition prompt

“You made some great observations! Now let’s discover how your ideas connect to today’s lesson.”
