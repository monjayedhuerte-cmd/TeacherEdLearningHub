CLASSROOM BRAIN BREAK GENERATOR — TEACHER ED

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.

FEATURES
- 16 ready-made brain breaks
- Random activity generator
- Filters: All, Movement, Brain, Focus, Fun, Calm
- Calm Energy and High Energy shortcuts
- 30 sec, 1 min, 2 min, 3 min timer
- Start, pause, resume, reset through activity selection
- Visual activity instructions
- Optional sound cue
- Fullscreen/projector mode
- Responsive design
- Offline; no database or server required

SAFETY
Always check the classroom space before movement activities. Keep students in personal space and avoid running, pushing, climbing, or unsafe jumping.

CUSTOMIZATION
Edit the activities array in script.js to add your own classroom brain breaks.
