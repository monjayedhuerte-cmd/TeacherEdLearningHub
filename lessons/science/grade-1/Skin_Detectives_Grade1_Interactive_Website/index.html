👩‍🏫 Teacher Ed's Quick Guide

  • Use the Mystery Bag to hook attention.
  • Let pupils describe real objects using touch words.
  • Use the interactive skin picture to introduce the parts.
  • Remind pupils never to touch very hot, sharp, or dangerous objects.
  • Ask: “How does your skin help you every day?”
