DO

Clean Gently

Use a clean, soft tissue, cotton, or handkerchief when cleaning your nose. Rough cloth can cause irritation.

DO

Protect Your Nose

When traveling, cover your nose with a clean tissue or handkerchief, or wear a nose mask to help avoid inhaling polluted air.

DON'T

Do Not Blow Hard

Blowing your nose too hard can hurt your nose as well as your ears.

ASK FOR HELP

See a Doctor

See a doctor at once if you can hardly breathe through your nose.

DON'T

Keep Small Objects Out

Do not put small objects inside your nose. You might inhale them and may injure your nose.

👃 Remember: Keep small objects away from your nose.

Five Nose-Care Rules

  1. Use a clean, soft tissue, cotton, or handkerchief.
  2. When traveling, cover your nose or wear a nose mask to help avoid polluted air.
  3. Do not put small objects inside your nose.
  4. Never blow your nose hard.
  5. See a doctor at once if you can hardly breathe through your nose.

📚 Quick Review

Everything important from the lesson in one place.

DO ✅

  • Use clean, soft materials.
  • Cover your nose when traveling in polluted air.
  • Wear a nose mask when appropriate.
  • Ask for medical help when breathing is very difficult.

DON'T 🚫

  • Do not put small objects inside your nose.
  • Do not blow your nose hard.
  • Avoid rough cloth that may irritate the nose.

Remember ⭐

Good nose care means being gentle, keeping harmful objects away, protecting your nose from polluted air, and getting help when needed.

Suggested checks
