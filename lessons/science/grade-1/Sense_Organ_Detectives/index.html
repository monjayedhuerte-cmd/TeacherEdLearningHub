🕵️ How to Play

  1. Open a detective station.
  2. Look, listen, smell, taste, or feel as the mission describes.
  3. Choose the sense organs that work together.
  4. Read the science clue after answering.
  5. Finish all five stations to earn your badge.
🛡️ Safety First: For the real classroom activity, use teacher-approved objects only. Never taste an unknown food or touch a hot, sharp, dirty, or unsafe object.
