# Science 1 — Taking Care of the Tongue, Teeth, and Mouth

Interactive primary lesson created from the textbook photos supplied by the teacher.

## Source-based content
The lesson content is based on the supplied Science Wonders 2 pages 73–74:
- Cool food before eating.
- Clean the tongue with a cotton swab or clean, soft cloth after each meal.
- Be careful not to bite the tongue.
- Avoid tasting unknown things because some may be toxic or poisonous.
- Brush teeth after every meal using a clean toothbrush with soft bristles.
- Use dental floss to remove particles between teeth.
- Use mouthwash when gargling.
- Avoid too much candy or sweet foods and brush after eating them.
- See a dentist at least once a year for a dental checkup.
