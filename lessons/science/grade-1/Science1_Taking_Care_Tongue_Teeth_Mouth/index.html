Our Big Idea

PHASE 2

📖 Teach — Learn in Small Chunks

The lesson source teaches several simple ways to care for the tongue, teeth, and mouth.

1. Cool Food First

Cool food before eating. Hot food could burn your tongue.

PROTECT YOUR TONGUE

2. Clean Your Tongue

Clean your tongue with a cotton swab or a clean, soft cloth after each meal.

KEEP IT CLEAN

3. Protect Your Tongue

Be careful not to bite your tongue.

BE CAREFUL

4. Never Taste Unknown Things

Avoid tasting all kinds of things. Some may be toxic or poisonous.

STAY SAFE

5. Brush Your Teeth

Brush your teeth after every meal. Use a clean toothbrush with soft bristles.

HEALTHY TEETH

6. Clean Between Teeth

Use dental floss to remove particles between your teeth. Use mouthwash when you gargle.

CLEAN BETWEEN

7. Limit Candy & Sweet Foods

Avoid eating too much candy or sweet foods. Brush your teeth after eating them.

SMART CHOICES

8. Visit the Dentist

See your dentist at least once a year for a dental checkup.
