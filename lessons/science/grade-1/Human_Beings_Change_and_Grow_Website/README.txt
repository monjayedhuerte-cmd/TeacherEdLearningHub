HUMAN BEINGS CHANGE AND GROW — INTERACTIVE SCIENCE WEBSITE
Teacher Ed Learning Hub | Teacher Ed | Grade 2 Science

HOW TO USE
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. The lesson section tabs are hidden by default. Click “Lesson Menu” to reveal them, then select a section.
4. Click “View Textbook Pages” to show or hide the six textbook photos.
5. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

FEATURES
- Connect, teach, teacher modeling, guided practice, independent practice, game zone, final challenge, and results.
- Four expandable concept cards: physical, mental, social, and emotional changes.
- Immediate feedback, hints, retry, stars, progress, best score, and localStorage persistence.
- Randomized game and final-quiz question order/choices.
- Responsive layout for phones, tablets, and computers.
- Optional speech toggle (browser support required).
- Supplied Teacher Ed Learning Hub logo and six textbook source photos.
- No backend and no student personal information collection.

Content is based on the user-provided Science Wonders 2 pages 87–91, “Human Beings Change and Grow.”
