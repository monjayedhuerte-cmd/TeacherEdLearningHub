📖 Learn: Eight Ear-Care Rules

Read each small lesson. Look at the picture, then tell yourself the safe habit.

1. Clean the outside

Clean the outside part of your ears with a clean, soft washcloth. The source also says cotton swabs may be used to clean the outside.

2. Keep hard objects out

Do not put pointed or hard objects such as hairpins or matchsticks into your ears.

3. Keep music volume low

Do not expose yourself to loud music or noise for a long time.

4. Do not shout into ears

Do not shout directly into another person's ears because it can hurt the ears.

5. Protect your ears from noise

Stay away from noisy places when possible. If you must be in a noisy place, use ear protection such as earplugs.

6. Be careful around very loud sounds

The source advises covering your ears and opening your mouth during a firecracker explosion.

7. Keep water out

When bathing or swimming, do not let water get into your ears.

8. Ask a doctor when there is a problem

See a doctor when you have an ear problem so your ears can be checked properly.

Remember: Clean the outside. Keep harmful objects and loud sounds away. Protect your ears. Ask a doctor when there is an ear problem.
