PHASE 1 • CONNECT

What do your senses help you do?

Think about everyday activities. Your sense organs often work together.

🍽️

Eating

Eyes see the food. Nose smells it. Tongue tastes it.

📖

Reading

Your eyes help you see the words in a book.

✍️

Writing

Your eyes help you see. Your skin helps you feel the paper and pen.

🦜

Playing

Depending on the game, several senses can help. You may see, hear, and feel.

PHASE 2 • TEACH

Meet the Five Sense Organs

Each sense organ has a special job. Together, they help you learn and stay safe.

👀

Eyes

Help you see colors, shapes, people, words, and objects.

SIGHT
👂

Ears

Help you hear sounds, voices, music, and warning signals.

HEARING
👃

Nose

Helps you smell food and things around you. Smelling happens when you breathe.

SMELL
👅

Tongue

Helps you taste food. It can tell sweet, sour, salty, and bitter tastes.

TASTE
🖐️

Skin

Helps you feel things such as hot, cold, soft, hard, rough, and smooth.

TOUCH
💡 Remember: Sense organs do not always work alone. Two or more can work together for one activity.
