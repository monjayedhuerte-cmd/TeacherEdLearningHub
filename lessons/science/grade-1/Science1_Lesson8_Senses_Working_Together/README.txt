SCIENCE 1 — LESSON 8: SENSE ORGANS WORKING TOGETHER
Teacher Ed Learning Hub

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/TeacherEdLogo.png

How to use:
1. Keep all files and the assets folder together.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. No internet connection or server is required.
4. The site saves the best final score in the browser's localStorage.

Content source:
User-provided Lesson 8 pages (Science Wonders 2 pages 54–61) covering sense organs working together: eyes, ears, nose, tongue, and skin; eating, reading, writing, playing, cooking, receiving/feeling a bird, crossing the street safely, smelling food/smoke, and the review concepts.

Design/teaching flow:
Connect → Teach → Model → Guided Practice → Independent Practice → Game Zone → Final Mastery Check → Remediation.

Important:
- No umami content.
- No frameworks; vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript.
- Large touch targets and responsive layout.
- Logo is used in header, footer, and favicon.
