⭐ The 5 Things You Must Remember

⏱️ 30-Second Review

SENSE ORGANS: Eyes, ears, nose, tongue, and skin.

WORK TOGETHER: When eating, the eyes see the food, the nose smells it, and the tongue tastes it.

CARE: Keep sense organs clean, protect them from harmful things, and ask an adult/doctor when help is needed.
