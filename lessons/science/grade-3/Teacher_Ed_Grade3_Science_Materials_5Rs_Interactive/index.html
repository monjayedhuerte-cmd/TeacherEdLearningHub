Lesson 6: Safe Use and Storage

Materials can help us—but they must be used properly.

Different materials are useful in daily household chores and in areas such as food and nutrition, agriculture, medicine, science, and technology. When used properly, materials can make life easier and more comfortable. When used irresponsibly, they can be harmful.

Useful material: a material that serves a helpful purpose when used properly.
Harmful material: a material that can cause injury, illness, pollution, or other harm when it is used or handled improperly.
Remember: Both useful and harmful materials can cause injury or death if swallowed or improperly handled. Proper storage and safe handling help protect people and the environment.

Safe storage rules

  • Keep containers out of children's reach; use high storage cabinets when appropriate.
  • Use child-safety locks or child-proof latches on floor-level cabinets in the kitchen and bathroom.
  • Keep materials in their original containers. Never put cleaning materials in food or drink containers.
  • Read warning labels. Keep flammable materials in a cool, dry place away from flames, ignition sources, and direct sunlight.
  • Never store acid solutions beside base solutions. Mixing incompatible materials may produce toxic fumes or even an explosion.
  • Use materials only where there is proper ventilation.
  • Never leave a bottle or container open and unattended. Seal it and put it away if work is interrupted.
  • Take only the amount needed, then seal the container and store it immediately.
  • Use protective equipment such as goggles or gloves when the label recommends it.
  • Dispose properly of objects that touched the materials. Some rags and paper towels may be very flammable.
  • Know what to do during an emergency and keep a first-aid kit nearby.

Know the warning signs

☠️

Poison

Warns that the material can be poisonous.

⚠️

Corrosive

Warns that the material can damage skin or other materials.

🔥

Flammable

Warns that the material can catch fire easily.

Safety habit: Read the label before using or storing a material. The label tells you important warnings and handling directions.

Lesson 7: The 5 Rs

A great way to conserve resources and help save the environment is to follow the 5 Rs: Recycle, Reduce, Reuse, Recover, and Repair.

♻️ Recycle

Make new products from old materials. Examples in the lesson include paper, steel and aluminum cans, plastics, and glass containers.

📉 Reduce

Use fewer resources and create less waste. Examples include choosing appropriate amounts and using energy-efficient products.

🔁 Reuse

Use an item again instead of throwing it away. Examples include refillable bottles, lunch boxes, reusable bags, cloth diapers, and suitable reused tires.

🔎 Recover

Collect and separate valuable materials from mixed waste so they can be used again. Material recovery facilities can recover glass, iron, aluminum, and other materials.

🛠️ Repair

Fix worn or damaged items so they can be used again, such as clothes, shoes, and bags.

Why are the 5 Rs important? They help conserve resources, reduce waste, and help protect the environment.
