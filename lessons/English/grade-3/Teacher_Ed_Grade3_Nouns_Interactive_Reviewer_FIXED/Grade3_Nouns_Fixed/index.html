📖 TEACH — Identifying Nouns

A noun is a name of a person, place, thing, animal, or event. In a sentence, the subject is the one that does the action or the one spoken of.

Person
teacher • mother • father • Rose
Place
Palawan • plaza • province • plane
Thing
ice cream • cotton candy • ferris wheel
Animal
hare • tortoise • fish • chicken • birds
Event
fiesta • Christmas Day • birthday

🏷️ Common and Proper Nouns

Common Noun

A common name of a person, place, thing, animal, or event. It begins with a small letter.

Examples: child, street, car, bird, birthday

Proper Noun

A specific name of a person, place, thing, animal, or event. It begins with a capital letter.

Examples: Charlene, Bulacan, Honda car, Blackie, Valentine's Day

➕ Regular and Irregular Nouns

Nouns may be singular (one) or plural (two or more).

Regular nouns form their plural by adding -s or -es at the end.

flower → flowers
boat → boats
bird → birds
table → tables
mango → mangoes
glass → glasses
house → houses
potato → potatoes

Rules for nouns ending in -y

1️⃣ Vowel before y → add -s: day → days, boy → boys, toy → toys, key → keys, clay → clays, bay → bays.
2️⃣ Consonant before y → change y to i and add -es: story → stories, party → parties, city → cities, sky → skies, diary → diaries, fairy → fairies.
