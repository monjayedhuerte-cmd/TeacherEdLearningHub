PHASE 1 • CONNECT

What do you already know?

Think about stories, words, and clues before you begin.

📚

What is a fable?

A fable is a story that often uses animals as characters and teaches a lesson or moral.

Think: What can a story teach us?

🔎

What is a context clue?

A context clue is a word or group of words that helps you understand an unfamiliar word.

Look around the word for helpful clues.

🔤

What is CVC?

CVC means Consonant–Vowel–Consonant, such as fun and him.

Listen for the middle vowel sound.

The Hare and the Tortoise

A hare and a tortoise decide to race to a mango tree. The hare is proud and thinks the race will be easy. He runs ahead, then sleeps under a tree. The tortoise keeps moving and eventually reaches the mango tree first.

Story lesson: Do not underestimate the ability of others.

WATCH ME • I DO

How a good reader finds the lesson

  1. Notice what each character does.
  2. Compare the hare's choice with the tortoise's choice.
  3. Ask: “What happened because of those choices?”
  4. State the lesson in your own simple words.

LESSON 6 • THE LOVELY MOUSE

Look beyond appearance.

The story shows that looks can be deceiving. The mouse is attracted to the handsome cat, but the cat later tries to eat her.

WORD WATCH

Important words

CONTEXT CLUES

Use nearby words

Example: “The lovely mouse … She looks more pleasing with her beautiful hat.”

The word beautiful helps us understand lovely.

Another clue: “The gallant cat … attract the attention of the lovely cat.” The nearby description helps us understand the word in context.
