🌱 Need Help?

Remember:

  • Nouns name people, places, things, animals, and events.
  • Common nouns usually begin with small letters.
  • Proper nouns name specific people, places, things, animals, or events and begin with capital letters.
  • For a noun ending in -y after a vowel, add -s: boy → boys.
  • For a noun ending in -y after a consonant, change -y to -i and add -es: city → cities.
