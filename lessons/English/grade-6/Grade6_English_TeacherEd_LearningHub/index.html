🧠 LEARN: Adverbs

An adverb can tell us more about an action. Many adverbs tell how an action happens.

Actionspoke
Adverbpolitely
Meaningin a polite way

Examples from the lesson include: happily, slowly, safely, quickly, softly, gradually, quietly, angrily, gratefully, greatly, tearfully, sadly, surely, impatiently, fearfully, wonderfully, breathlessly, undoubtedly, haughtily, fearfully.

🗣️ LEARN: Sample Telephone Conversation

John: Hello, good morning. This is John Rivera. May I speak with Mrs. Ramona Deloso?

Judy: I’m sorry, Mr. Rivera. Mrs. Deloso is in the meeting today. Would you like to leave a message?

John: Yes, please. Could you ask her to call me back later? We have important matters to discuss.

Judy: Okay, I’ll give her the message.

John: Thank you very much!

Judy: You’re welcome!

Useful expressions

  • May I speak with…?
  • Would you like to leave a message?
  • Could you ask her/him to call me back?
  • Thank you very much.
  • You’re welcome.

Why they work

They are clear, respectful, and appropriate for a formal telephone conversation.

🎬 LEARN: Film Blocking

Blocking is the relationship of the camera to the actors. It includes the physical movement of actors in relation to the camera.

Why?Why does the actor move?
Where?Where does the actor move?
When?When does the actor move?
How?How does the actor move?

Good blocking should help the audience understand the central idea, the story, and what the characters are feeling. Actor movement should have a purpose.

🔄 REVIEW

What Should You Remember?

⭐ Remember This

  • Adverbs can add information about an action, often telling how it happens.
  • Polite telephone language makes communication respectful and clear.
  • Blocking gives purpose to actor movement in relation to the camera.

🧠 Vocabulary

  • adverb — a word that adds information about an action or another word.
  • blocking — planned actor movement and position in relation to the camera.
  • message — information given to another person.

📌 Quick Rules

  • Choose words that fit the meaning and situation.
  • Formal calls use respectful expressions.
  • Movement in a scene should contribute to the story or idea.

💡 Common Mistakes

❌ “Put her on.” in a formal call.

✅ “May I speak with her, please?”

❌ Moving an actor without a purpose.

✅ Make movement support the scene.

🌍 Where Can I Use This?

Use these skills when making phone calls, speaking respectfully, writing stories, describing actions, reading scripts, creating videos, or explaining how a scene communicates an idea.

🏆 FINAL MISSION

English Communication Boss Challenge

Read the situation

You are making a short video about a student who needs to call an office. The student must speak politely, use an adverb to describe an action, and plan meaningful movement for the scene.

  1. Write one polite opening for the telephone call.
  2. Write one sentence using an adverb.
  3. Explain one reason why the actor might move during the scene.
Sample answers:
1. “Hello, good morning. May I speak with Mrs. Deloso, please?”
2. “She spoke politely to the caller.”
3. “The actor may move to show a change in focus, feeling, or action in the story.”
Your answers may be different if they are clear and appropriate.
