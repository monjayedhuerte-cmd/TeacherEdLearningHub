Big Question

Why must subjects and verbs agree?

Because the subject and verb need to match in number—singular or plural.

Singular subject → singular verb
Plural subject → plural verb
02 • Learn

Subject–Verb Agreement

The main grammar skill from the lesson.

🧠 The Rule

A singular subject takes a singular verb, while a plural subject takes a plural verb.

SubjectVerbExample
Mr. LunahelpsMr. Luna helps keep the barangay clean.
PeoplethrowPeople throw the garbage in the pit.
MotherlovesMother loves to cook pastries.
Bright flowersattractBright flowers attract bees and butterflies.

👀 A Helpful Pattern

One person/thing: The captain visits the barangay.
More than one: Doctors and nurses talk to the people.
💡
Tip: First find the subject. Then ask: “Is it one or more than one?” Choose a verb that matches.

👩‍🏫 I Do — Worked Example

Sentence: Everybody (live, lives) happily in the community.

  1. Find the subject: Everybody.
  2. “Everybody” refers to each person one at a time, so it is treated as singular.
  3. Choose the singular verb: lives.
✅ Everybody lives happily in the community.

06 • Review

Quick Review

Save these ideas in your grammar toolkit.

⭐ Remember

Singular subject → singular verb.

Plural subject → plural verb.

💡 Watch Out

Words like everybody are treated as singular.

Example: Everybody lives happily.

🐢 Reading Link

In informational writing, verbs must still agree with their subjects—even when the sentence contains extra details.

Folklore Connection
