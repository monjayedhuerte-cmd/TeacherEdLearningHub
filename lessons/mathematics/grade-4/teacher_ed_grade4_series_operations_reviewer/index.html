MAKE IT STICK

Memory Booster

Keep these rules in mind as you solve.

↔

Left to right

When an expression has only addition and subtraction, work from left to right.

18 − 7 + 4 = 11 + 4 = 15
括

Grouping first

If parentheses or other grouping symbols appear, calculate inside them first.

20 − (6 + 3) = 20 − 9 = 11
.

Align decimals

For decimal addition or subtraction, line up decimal points and add zeros when needed.

5.4 + 2.35 = 5.40 + 2.35 = 7.75
₱

Word problems

Identify what is known and what must be found. Write an expression before calculating.

Change = amount paid − total cost
Memory cue: “GROUPS FIRST; OTHERWISE, LEFT TO RIGHT.”
Why can 12 − 4 + 3 not be solved by adding 4 + 3 first?

There are no grouping symbols. Addition and subtraction have equal priority in this lesson, so follow the expression from left to right: 12 − 4 = 8, then 8 + 3 = 11.

BEFORE YOU GO

Final Memory Lock-In

  1. Grouping symbols: solve inside them first.
  2. Addition and subtraction: when there are no grouping symbols, work from left to right.
  3. Decimals: align decimal points; annex zeros when useful.
  4. Word problems: translate the situation into an expression before calculating.
  5. Check your work: estimate or use the context to see whether the answer makes sense.
