Decimal Place Value

Decimals are easier to add or subtract when each digit is placed in the correct column.

Remember this

Line up decimal points. Ones go under ones, tenths under tenths, hundredths under hundredths, and thousandths under thousandths.

WORKED EXAMPLE
56.932+ 0.638
57.570

Annex a zero when helpful: 56.932 + 0.638 = 57.570 = 57.57.

★

Math strategy: Think about place value first. Estimate whether your answer should be larger or smaller before calculating.
