DEPED ALIGNMENT

Content & Performance Standards

Content Standard

The learners should have knowledge and understanding of:

  • volume of pyramids, cones, and spheres;
  • relationships among dimensions and volume of solid figures;
  • formulas and appropriate units for volume;
  • mathematical reasoning in real-life measurement situations.

Performance Standard

By the end of the term, the learners are able to:

  • explore inductively the volumes of cones and spheres;
  • find the volumes of cones and spheres;
  • explain how the formulas are connected to known solids;
  • solve contextual problems involving volumes of cones and spheres.
Week 2 Learning Competency: Explore inductively the volumes of cones and spheres, leading to their formulas; find the volumes of cones and spheres; and solve problems involving their volumes.

INTERACTIVE EXPLORATION

Formula Lab

Change the dimensions and immediately see how volume changes.

Cone Explorer

V = ⅓πr²h
V = ⅓πr²h
150.80 cm³

Compare this with a cylinder having the same radius and height. The cone should have one-third of the cylinder's volume.

Sphere Explorer

V = ⁴⁄₃πr³
V = ⁴⁄₃πr³
523.60 cm³

The sphere is related to a cylinder of radius r and height 2r. The sphere occupies two-thirds of that cylinder's volume.
