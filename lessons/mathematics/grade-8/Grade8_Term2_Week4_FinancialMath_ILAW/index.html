Warm-Up: The Weekend Job

You help at a small school event and receive ₱450 for 5 hours of work. What does that mean?

L — LEARN

1. Earnings and Rate of Pay

Earnings are the money received from work or a service. If pay is based on time, we often use:

Total Earnings = Rate × Time
Example A

₱80/hour × 6 hours = ₱480.

Example B

₱650 for 5 days = ₱130/day.

650 ÷ 5 = 130
Example C

₱120/hour for 7.5 hours = ₱900.

120 × 7.5 = 900
Think: What quantity is missing? Rate, time, or total earnings? Choose the operation that connects the three.
A — APPLY

Q2. Ben earned ₱840 in 7 hours. What was his hourly rate?

Q3. A student earns ₱75/hour. How many hours are needed to earn ₱600?

DAY 2

Profit and Loss: “Did the Seller Win or Lose?”

50-minute interactive class

⏱ 0–7 min⏱ 7–23 min⏱ 23–43 min⏱ 43–50 min
I — INTRODUCE

Market Challenge

A seller buys a notebook for ₱45 and sells it for ₱60. Is that a profit or a loss?

L — LEARN

Cost Price, Selling Price, Profit, and Loss

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price
Loss = Cost Price − Selling Price
Profit Example

CP = ₱250, SP = ₱310

₱310 − ₱250 = ₱60 profit

Loss Example

CP = ₱500, SP = ₱430

₱500 − ₱430 = ₱70 loss

1 Identify CP and SP. 2 Compare them. 3 Subtract the smaller amount from the larger. 4 Label the result profit or loss.
A — APPLY

Q3. A seller buys a bag for ₱640 and wants a ₱160 profit. What should the selling price be?

Same Product, Different Package

Store A sells 3 notebooks for ₱ ninety (₱90). Store B sells 5 notebooks for ₱140. Which is cheaper per notebook?

L — LEARN

Unit Price: The Secret of a Best Buy

When packages have different sizes, compare the cost per 1 unit.

Unit Price = Total Price ÷ Number of Units
Deal A

6 pens = ₱72

₱72 ÷ 6 = ₱12/pen
Deal B

10 pens = ₱110

₱110 ÷ 10 = ₱11/pen

Deal B is the better buy because each pen costs ₱11 instead of ₱12.

Important: A bigger package is not automatically the best buy. Always compare unit prices.

DAY 4

Buying on Terms: “Cash or Instalment?”

50-minute interactive class

⏱ 0–8 min⏱ 8–25 min⏱ 25–43 min⏱ 43–50 min
I — INTRODUCE

The Phone Deal

A phone costs ₱12,000 cash. Another offer is ₱2,500 down payment plus 5 monthly payments of ₱2,100. Which question should a smart buyer ask first?

L — LEARN

Buying on Terms / Instalment Plan

In an instalment plan, the price is paid in parts. To compare it fairly with the cash price, find the total amount paid.

Total Instalment Cost = Down Payment + (Number of Payments × Payment per Period)
Worked Example

Cash price = ₱12,000

Down payment = ₱2,500

5 payments × ₱2,100 = ₱10,500

Total = ₱2,500 + ₱10,500 = ₱13,000

Compared with cash, the instalment plan costs ₱1,000 more.

Smart-buyer rule: Never judge a deal from the monthly payment alone. Calculate the whole cost.
A — APPLY

Instalment Investigator

Q1. A laptop has a ₱3,000 down payment and 8 monthly payments of ₱1,500. What is the total instalment cost?

Q2. The cash price is ₱14,000. If total instalment cost is ₱15,200, how much more is paid through instalment?

Q3. A bicycle costs ₱9,500 cash or ₱1,500 down + 4 payments of ₱2,100. Which is cheaper?

Four Skills, One Smart Decision

💵 EarningHow much money is received?
📈 Profit/LossDid SP exceed or fall below CP?
🛒 Best BuyWhich has the lower unit price?
🧾 InstalmentWhat is the total amount paid?
L — LEARN

Decision Checklist

  1. Underline the important numbers and units.
  2. Identify what the question asks.
  3. Choose the correct relationship or formula.
  4. Compute carefully and show the operation.
  5. Label the answer and ask: “Does it make sense?”
