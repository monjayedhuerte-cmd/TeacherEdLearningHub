Systems of Linear Equations in Two Variables

This week, you will learn how two linear equations can work together to describe a situation—and how graphing can help you find their common solution.

📈➕📈

Your Week 1 Mission

By the end of the week, you should be able to:

  • define and illustrate a system of linear equations in two variables;
  • recognize that a solution to a system must satisfy both equations;
  • solve systems with integer solutions by graphing; and
  • explain what the intersection of two lines means.

🎯 Learning Competencies

1. Define and illustrate a system of linear equations in two variables.
2. Solve a system of linear equations (with integer solutions) by graphing.

DAY 1 • ILAWINTRODUCE + LEARN50 min

What Is a System of Linear Equations?

⏱ Suggested flow: 5 min hook • 10 min discussion • 15 min guided examples • 15 min interactive practice • 5 min exit check

🚕 Hook: Two Clues, One Answer

A school fair sells regular tickets for ₱40 and student tickets for ₱25. A group buys 10 tickets for a total of ₱310.

Question: Could you determine how many of each type were bought? What two facts could become equations?

📘 Learn: The Big Idea

A system of linear equations in two variables is a set of two or more linear equations involving the same variables. In this week, we focus on systems of two equations in two variables.

x + y = 1040x + 25y = 310

Both equations describe the same situation. A solution must make both equations true at the same time.

Remember: “System” means we consider the equations together—not separately.

🔎 What Does a Solution Mean?

An ordered pair (x, y) is a solution of the system if it satisfies every equation in the system.

Example
x + y = 10
x − y = 2
Try (6, 4)
6 + 4 = 10 ✓
6 − 4 = 2 ✓
Therefore
(6, 4) is the solution.

⚡ Check Your Understanding

Which statement is true?

DAY 2 • ILAWLEARN + APPLY50 min

Illustrating a System as Two Lines

⏱ Suggested flow: 5 min review • 10 min concept • 15 min table/graph construction • 15 min guided practice • 5 min exit check

🔗 Connect Equations to Graphs

Each linear equation can be represented by a straight line. When two equations are graphed on the same coordinate plane, we can look for a common point.

y = x + 1y = −x + 5

Make a value table for each equation, plot the points, then draw the lines. The point where the two lines meet is the candidate for the system's solution.

🧩 Guided Example

For y = x + 1, when x = 0, y = 1. When x = 2, y = 3.

For y = −x + 5, when x = 0, y = 5. When x = 2, y = 3.

Both lines contain (2, 3) → the system's solution is (2, 3).

🎯 Interactive: Find the Common Point

System: y = x + 2 and y = −x + 6. What ordered pair lies on both lines?

💡 Substitute the x-value into both equations. For x = 2, both give y = 4.

🧠 Think Like a Mathematician

Why is the intersection important? Write or say your answer: “The intersection represents __________ because __________.”

DAY 3 • ILAWAPPLY50 min

Solve a System by Graphing

⏱ Suggested flow: 5 min warm-up • 10 min teacher model • 15 min guided graphing • 15 min independent interactive practice • 5 min exit ticket

🪜 The 5-Step Graphing Method

  1. Write both equations clearly.
  2. Make a table of convenient x-values and find y-values.
  3. Plot at least two points for each line.
  4. Draw each straight line.
  5. Read the intersection as the ordered-pair solution.

🧮 Worked Example

Solve by graphing:

y = x + 1y = −x + 5

Line 1: points can include (0,1) and (2,3).

Line 2: points can include (0,5) and (2,3).

Both lines pass through (2,3).

✅ Solution: (2, 3)

🎮 Practice Round

Which point is the solution of y = 2x + 1 and y = −x + 7?

⚠️ Notice the choices carefully: verify by substitution. For (2,5), first equation gives 5 and second gives 5.

🔍 Verification Challenge

Is (3,4) a solution of the system y = x + 1 and y = −x + 7?

Use Systems to Describe Real Situations

⏱ Suggested flow: 5 min review • 10 min problem setup • 15 min graph interpretation • 15 min problem-solving • 5 min reflection

🏫 Real-Life Problem

A school club sells adult passes for ₱50 and student passes for ₱30. They sell 12 passes and collect ₱480.

Let x = adult passes and y = student passes.

x + y = 1250x + 30y = 480

Graphing the two equations lets us find the pair that satisfies both facts.

🎯 Choose the Meaning of the Solution

If the graph shows the intersection (3, 9), what does it mean?

🧠 Error Detective
