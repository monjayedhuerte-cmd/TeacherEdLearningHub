HOW TO USE:
1. Keep all files in the same folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or another modern browser.
3. Use the DAY buttons to present one 50-minute lesson at a time.
4. Use Full Screen for projector/presentation mode.
5. Day 5 contains a 20-item automatic assessment.

CURRICULUM BASIS:
Second Term Week 3 in the supplied Grade 8 Mathematics Budget of Work:
- Apply the Pythagorean Theorem in finding the missing side of a right triangle, and its converse in classifying triangles.
- Apply the triangle inequality theorems to establish results for angles and sides in triangles.
