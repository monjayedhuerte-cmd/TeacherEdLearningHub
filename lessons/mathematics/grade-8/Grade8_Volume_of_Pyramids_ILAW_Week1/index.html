FORMULA LAB

Derive the Pyramid Volume Formula

Do not memorize first. Understand where the one-third comes from.

1

Start with a prism

A prism with the same base area B and perpendicular height h has volume:

Vprism = Bh
↓
2

Compare three equal-height pyramids

When a prism is decomposed into three pyramidal regions with the same base area and height relationship, the pyramid occupies one-third of the prism's volume.

Vpyramid = ⅓Vprism
↓
3

Substitute the prism volume

Since the prism volume is Bh, replace it in the relationship:

V = ⅓(Bh)
↓
✓

Final formula

V = ⅓Bh

B = area of the base   •   h = perpendicular height

⚠️ Height reminder: In the formula, h is the perpendicular height from the apex straight down to the base. It is not automatically the slant height.
