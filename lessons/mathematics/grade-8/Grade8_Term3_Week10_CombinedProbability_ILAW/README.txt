WEEK 10 LEARNING COMPETENCY
Calculate the probability of simple combined events by listing, or by possibility diagrams or tree diagrams.

CONTENT STANDARD CONNECTION
The Third Term includes experimental and theoretical probability and the Fundamental Counting Principle.

PERFORMANCE STANDARD CONNECTION
By the end of the term, learners are expected to calculate the probability of a single event and the probability of simple combined events.
