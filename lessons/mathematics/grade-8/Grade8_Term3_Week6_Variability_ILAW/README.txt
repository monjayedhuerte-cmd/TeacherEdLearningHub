WEEK 6 COMPETENCIES
1. Calculate the measures of variability (range, mean deviation, and standard deviation) for ungrouped data.
2. Draw conclusions from statistical data using the measures of variability.

CONTENT STANDARD
The Grade 8 Third Term content standard includes knowledge and understanding of measures of variability for ungrouped data.

PERFORMANCE STANDARD
By the end of the term, learners are able to calculate measures of variability for ungrouped data and use statistical measures to draw conclusions.
