GRADE 1 SHAPES — ILAW INTERACTIVE LESSON
============================================

Files:
- index.html  : Main lesson structure/content
- style.css   : Complete responsive visual design
- script.js   : All interactive activities and scoring

How to use:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep index.html, style.css, and script.js in the same folder.
3. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or another modern browser.
4. Internet is only needed for the Google Fonts import. The activities themselves use HTML/CSS/JavaScript.

Lesson alignment:
I — Introduce: Shape Hunt and real-life objects
L — Learn: Shape features, orientation, comparison
A — Apply: Shape Detective, Shape Sorter, Shape Builder, Decomposition
W — Wrap Up: Mastery score, stars, reflection

Learning competencies:
1. Identify simple 2-dimensional shapes (triangle, rectangle, square) of different size and orientation.
2. Compare and distinguish 2-dimensional shapes according to sides and corners.
3. Compose and decompose triangles, squares, and rectangles.

Teacher note:
This is a classroom-ready interactive lesson resource. The teacher can guide learners orally while learners interact with the visual activities.
