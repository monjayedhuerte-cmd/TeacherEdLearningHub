📚 WORD POWER

Math Words We Need

AddendA number being added to another number.
SumThe answer in an addition problem.
EstimateA number close to the exact value.
RoundChange a number to a nearby easier number.
Estimated SumThe approximate answer found by estimating.
Place ValueThe value of a digit based on its position.
Exact SumThe actual answer when we add exactly.
ReasonableAn answer that makes sense and is close enough.
⚡ Quick Check: In 347 + 251, what are the addends?

How Do We Estimate a Sum?

1
Choose

Choose the place value to round to.

2
Look

Look at the digit to the right.

3
Round

5 or more: round up. 4 or less: keep it.

4
Add

Add the rounded numbers.
