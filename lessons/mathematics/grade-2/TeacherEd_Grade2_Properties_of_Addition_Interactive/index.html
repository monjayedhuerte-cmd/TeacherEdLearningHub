04 • LEARN

Three Rules That Make Addition Easier

Teacher-guided discussion: show → ask → think → explain → apply.

CONCEPTEXAMPLEWHY?APPLY

Commutative

3 + 7 = 7 + 3

Order changes; the total does not.

Ask: “Did the amount change, or only the order?”

Associative

(3 + 7) + 2 = 3 + (7 + 2)

Grouping changes; the total does not.

Ask: “Which two addends are we combining first?”

Identity

9 + 0 = 9

Adding zero leaves the number unchanged.

Ask: “What happens when nothing is added?”
