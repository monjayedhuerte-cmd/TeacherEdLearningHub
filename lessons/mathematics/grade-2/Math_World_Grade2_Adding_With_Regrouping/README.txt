MATH WORLD — GRADE 2: ADDING NUMBERS WITH REGROUPING

Open index.html in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Lesson sequence:
1. Welcome to Math World
2. Shopping Hook
3. Math Vocabulary
4. Regrouping Lab
5. LINE → START → ADD → REGROUP → CHECK
6. Guided Practice
7. Team Shop
8. Mastery Mission
9. Generalize
10. Exit Mission

Features:
- Teacher-led interactive lesson
- Strong motivation/hook based on the textbook's classroom-shopping activity
- Visual regrouping lab: 10 ones -> 1 ten
- Vocabulary instruction
- 4- to 6-digit addition
- Up to four addends
- Interactive answer checks and XP
- Team shopping activity
- Advanced learner challenges
- Teacher notes for heterogeneous classes including autism and ADHD
- Focus Mode for reduced visual stimulation
- Sound toggle
- Responsive design for projector, laptop, tablet and phone
- No student data is stored or transmitted

Recommended classroom use:
Project the website and control the pace as the teacher. Do not let students race through the pages independently; pause at the Hook, Regrouping Lab, Guided Practice and Generalization sections.
