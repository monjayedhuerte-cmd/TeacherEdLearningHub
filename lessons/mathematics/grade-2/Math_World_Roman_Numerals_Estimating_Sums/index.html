Station 4: Estimation Lab

Estimation helps us find a close answer quickly.

4-step strategy:
1. Find the place to round.
2. Look at the digit to its right.
3. 5 or more? Round up. 4 or less? Keep it.
4. Add the rounded numbers.

Example: 348 + 251 → 350 + 250 → 600
