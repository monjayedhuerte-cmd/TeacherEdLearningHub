4-Digit Roman Numerals

For this reviewer, 4-digit Roman numerals are numbers from 1,000 to 3,999.

1,000 = M
1,245 = MCCXLV
2,024 = MMXXIV
3,999 = MMMCMXCIX

Build the number by representing the thousands, hundreds, tens, and ones.

WORLD 2: ESTIMATING SUMS

THE GOLDEN RULE: Round each addend to its highest place value before adding.

Example: 1,245 + 2,331

1,245 → 1,000 because it is a 4-digit number.

2,331 → 2,000 because it is a 4-digit number.

1,000 + 2,000 = 3,000

3-digit: hundreds   |   4-digit: thousands   |   5-digit: ten-thousands   |   6-digit: hundred-thousands
