Roman Numeral Symbols

Examples

  • 8 = VIII
  • 14 = XIV
  • 25 = XXV
  • 40 = XL
  • 50 = L
  • 100 = C
  • 2,024 = MMXXIV
  • 3,999 = MMMCMXCIX

Estimating the Sum

Round EACH addend to its HIGHEST PLACE VALUE.
  1. Find the highest place value.
  2. Look at the digit to its right.
  3. 5 or more → round up.
  4. 4 or less → keep it.
  5. Add the rounded numbers.
348 + 251 → 300 + 300 → 600
