Learn: Three Smart Strategies

Strategy 1: Make a friendly ten

Break a number apart to make the next ten. This can make mental addition easier.

7
52
25 + 7

25 + 5 + 2

30 + 2 = 32

Singapore Math: part–part–whole number bonds help us see how a number can be split.

Strategy 2: Use compatible numbers

Compatible numbers are numbers that are easy to add mentally.

44
404
12 + 23 + 44

12 + 23 = 35

35 + 44 = 79

Look for pairs or groups that make a friendly ten or hundred.

Strategy 3: Use properties of addition

🔄 Commutative

Switch the addends.

7 + 4 = 4 + 7The sum stays 11.
🧺 Associative

Change the grouping.

(2 + 8) + 3 = 2 + (8 + 3)The sum stays 13.
🪄 Identity

Add zero. The number stays the same.

76 + 0 = 76Zero changes nothing.

Watch Me: Add Mentally

EXAMPLE

27 + 43 = ?

Step 1: Break 27 into 20 + 7 and 43 into 40 + 3.
27+43
Step 1 of 4

Think in parts: tens first, then ones. 20 + 40 = 60 and 7 + 3 = 10, so 60 + 10 = 70.
