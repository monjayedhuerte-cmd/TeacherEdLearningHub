🔢 Metric Prefixes

🧭 The Direction Rule

When converting to a smaller unit, the numerical value usually gets larger. When converting to a larger unit, the numerical value usually gets smaller.

2 m → 200 cm ×100
2,000 g → 2 kg ÷1,000

🌎 Across Systems

Some tasks require conversion between metric/SI and customary units. Use the given conversion factor, multiply or divide carefully, and keep the unit in your working.

1 inch = 2.54 cm exact
1 foot = 30.48 cm exact
