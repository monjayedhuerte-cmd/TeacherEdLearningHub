ILAW TEACHING STRATEGY

Our 4-Step Learning Path

Each day uses a simple cycle: connect, understand, use, and reflect.

I

Introduce

Start with a situation students can imagine: floors, temperature, scores, distance, and directions.

L

Learn

Build understanding through number lines, visual models, rules, examples, and guided thinking.

A

Apply

Use the skill in interactive questions, comparisons, ordering tasks, and real-life problems.

W

Wrap-Up

Check what you know, explain your thinking, fix mistakes, and prepare for the next step.
