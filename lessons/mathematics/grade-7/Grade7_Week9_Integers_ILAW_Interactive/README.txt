Topic:
Number and Algebra — Integers

Learning Competencies:
1. Describe the set of integers.
2. Use positive and negative numbers to describe directions or opposites in real-life situations.

Content Standard:
The learners demonstrate understanding of the set of integers and how positive and negative numbers represent opposites in real-life situations.

Performance Standard:
The learners use integers to describe, represent, compare, and explain directions or opposite situations accurately.

Teaching Strategy:
ILAW — Introduce, Learn, Apply, Wrap-Up.

Schedule:
Day 1 — Meet the Integers
Day 2 — Number Line & Opposites
Day 3 — Positive & Negative in Real Life
Day 4 — Compare & Order Integers
Day 5 — Review, Explain & Mastery

Each day is structured for a 50-minute class:
- Introduce: motivation / prior knowledge
- Learn: explicit explanation and examples
- Apply: interactive questions and practice
- Wrap-Up: summary / exit thinking

The website includes a 20-item automatically scored assessment and a working browser Full Screen button.

Branding:
Keep BrandLogo.png in the same folder as index.html. It is used in the header, footer, and browser-tab favicon.
