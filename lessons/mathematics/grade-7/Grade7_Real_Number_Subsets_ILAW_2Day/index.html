The Real Number Family Tree 🌳

Think of real numbers as a big family. Smaller number sets live inside larger sets.

REAL NUMBERS ℝ

All numbers that can be located on the number line.

RATIONAL NUMBERS ℚ

Numbers that can be written as a/b, where a and b are integers and b ≠ 0.

INTEGERS ℤ

Negative numbers, zero, and positive counting numbers.

WHOLE NUMBERS 𝕎

0 and the positive counting numbers.

NATURAL NUMBERS ℕ

1, 2, 3, 4, …

IRRATIONAL NUMBERS 𝕀Cannot be written as a ratio of two integers. Examples: √2, π.

DAY 2

Rational, Irrational, and Real Numbers

🔢 Rational vs. Irrational

Rational numbers can be expressed as a quotient of two integers. Their decimal forms either terminate or repeat.

  • 1/4 = 0.25 → terminates → rational
  • 2/3 = 0.666… → repeats → rational
  • √2 = 1.414213… → non-terminating and non-repeating → irrational
  • π = 3.14159… → non-terminating and non-repeating → irrational
