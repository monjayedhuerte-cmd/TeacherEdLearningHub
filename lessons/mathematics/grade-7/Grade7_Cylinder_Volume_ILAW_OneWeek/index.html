INDUCTIVE DISCOVERY

From what we know to what we can conclude

Area of circleπr²
Height of cylinderh
Volumeπr² × h = πr²h
V = πr²h

Meaning: volume is the area of the circular base multiplied by the perpendicular height.

📦 Volume is measured in cubic units: cm³, m³, in³, and so on.
