How to Do the Graded Recitation

  1. Read the question carefully.
  2. Say your answer aloud in your own words.
  3. Click Show Guide Answer after you answer.
  4. Compare your answer with the guide answer.
  5. If your answer is correct or shows the same idea, click Correct.
  6. If you need to improve your answer, click Needs Improvement.
  7. Continue until all 30 questions are completed.
  8. Take a screenshot of your final score for submission.
