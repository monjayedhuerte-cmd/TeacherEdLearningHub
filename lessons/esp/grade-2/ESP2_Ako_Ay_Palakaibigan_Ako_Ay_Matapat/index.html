Stage 3 • Memory Booster

Mga Dapat Tandaan

Important things to remember
🤝

Palakaibigan Friendly

Pagiging palakaibigan ay pagiging mabait, magiliw, at handang makipag-ugnayan sa iba.

Being friendly means being kind, welcoming, and willing to connect with others.
Memory Cue: “Bati • Tulong • Ngiti • Tanggap.”Greet • Help • Smile • Welcome.
🫱

Matapat Honest

Pagiging matapat ay pagsasabi ng totoo at pagiging tapat sa salita at gawa.

Being honest means telling the truth and being truthful in words and actions.
Memory Cue: “Sabihin ang totoo.”“Tell the truth.”

Pagbati Greeting

Maayos na pagbati sa kaibigan, kaklase, bisita, o bagong kakilala.

A polite greeting to a friend, classmate, visitor, or new acquaintance.

Pagtulong Helping

Pagtulong sa iba kapag kaya at angkop.

Helping others when you can and when it is appropriate.

Katapatan Honesty

Pagsasabi ng totoo, pag-amin sa pagkakamali, at paggalang sa pag-aari ng iba.

Telling the truth, admitting mistakes, and respecting other people's property.
Stage 4 • Remember It!

Think of it this way

PALAKAIBIGAN = BUKAS SA IBA

FRIENDLY = OPEN AND KIND TO OTHERS

Kapag palakaibigan ka, ipinapakita mo ang kabaitan sa pakikipag-ugnayan sa iba.

When you are friendly, you show kindness in the way you interact with others.
Memory Lock

MATAPAT = TOTOO

HONEST = TRUTHFUL

Kapag may nagawa kang mali, mas mabuting magsabi ng totoo at umako sa ginawa.

When you make a mistake, it is better to tell the truth and take responsibility for what you did.
