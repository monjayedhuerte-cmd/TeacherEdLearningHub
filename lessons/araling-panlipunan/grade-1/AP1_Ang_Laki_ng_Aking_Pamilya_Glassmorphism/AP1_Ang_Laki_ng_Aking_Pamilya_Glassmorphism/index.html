👨‍👩‍👧 Ano ang pamilya?

Ang pamilya ay mga taong nagmamahalan, nag-aalaga, at nagtutulungan. Hindi kailangang pare-pareho ang lahat ng pamilya.

A family is made up of people who love, care for, and help one another. Families do not have to look the same.
👨‍👩‍👧

Two-Parent Family

Binubuo ng nanay, tatay, at anak o mga anak.

A family with two parents and one or more children.
👩 + 👨 + 👧 = Two-Parent
👩‍👧

Solo-Parent Family

Isang magulang ang nag-aalaga at gumagabay sa anak o mga anak.

A family where one parent cares for and guides the child or children.
👩 + 👦 = Solo-Parent
👨‍👩‍👧‍👵

Extended Family

Maaaring kasama ang lolo, lola, tito, tita, at iba pang kamag-anak.

A family that may include grandparents, aunts, uncles, and other relatives.
👩 + 👨 + 👧 + 👵 = Extended
❤️ Mahalagang Paalala: Ang solo-parent family ay pamilya rin at mahalaga. Kahit maliit o malaki ang pamilya, mahalaga ang pagmamahalan at pagtutulungan.A solo-parent family is also a family and is important. Whether a family is small or large, love and cooperation matter.

🎲 Quick Class Call-out

Kapag sinabi ko ang isang bagay, sabay-sabay ninyong sabihin: “Pangangailangan!” kung kailangan upang mabuhay nang maayos, at “Hindi pangunahing pangangailangan!” kung hindi.

When I name an item, say “Need!” if it is necessary for living well, or “Not a basic need!” if it is not.
