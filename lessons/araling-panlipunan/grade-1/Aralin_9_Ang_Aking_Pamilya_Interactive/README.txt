# Aralin 9 – Ang Aking Pamilya
Interactive Araling Panlipunan lesson website for Grade 1.

## Files
- index.html – complete lesson structure/content
- styles.css – child-friendly responsive design
- script.js – games, matching activity, family tree builder, quiz, navigation

## How to use
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or another modern browser.
3. No server is required.
4. Internet is only needed if you want the Google Fonts to load; the lesson itself works without a server.

## Lesson flow
1. Motivation – Family Mystery
2. Tuklasin – Kahulugan ng pamilya and family types
3. Talakayin – Family members and roles
4. Gawin – Matching Game
5. Pagpapalalim – Family Tree
6. Pagtataya – 7-item interactive quiz
7. Pagpapahalaga – Family Appreciation Circle
8. Teacher Mode – quick teaching notes

The content is based on the photographed reference pages supplied in the conversation and the DLL flow created from them.
