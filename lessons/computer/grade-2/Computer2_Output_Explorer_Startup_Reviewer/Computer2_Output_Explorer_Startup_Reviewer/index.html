Teacher Ed says:

An output device is hardware that sends information from the computer to the user.

🖥️

Monitor

Displays text, pictures, and videos. Also called a screen.

🖨️

Printer

Produces a hard copy of information on paper.

🔊

Speaker

Produces sound from the computer.

🎧

Headset

Used for listening and speaking; it has a microphone.

When you press the power button, the computer performs several steps before the desktop is ready.
