Protect your computer, one idea at a time.

Click a card to reveal the explanation.

🦠

Malware

Software designed to damage a computer or gain unauthorized access.

Viruses, worms, Trojan horses, and spyware are examples.
🛡️

Antivirus

Software that helps protect a computer from malware.

It can help prevent malware from being installed and can remove malware.
🌐

Stay Smart Online

Think before clicking or downloading.

Avoid suspicious websites, links, and email attachments. Antivirus may not catch everything.
💾

Backup

Keep another copy of important files.

Back up to an external hard drive or an online backup service.
🔐

Personal Information

Protect information from unauthorized access.

Use user IDs and passwords and keep sensitive information private.
🧹

Maintenance

Keep files and folders uncluttered.

Delete unwanted files and use tools such as Disk Cleanup to free space.

THINK → PROTECT → BACK UP → MAINTAIN

Your safest habit is to think before you click, protect your computer, keep copies of important files, and maintain an organized system.
