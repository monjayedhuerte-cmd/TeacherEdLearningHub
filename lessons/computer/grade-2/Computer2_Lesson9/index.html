CORE IDEA

Why is computer security important?

Computer security is important because it keeps your information protected. It is also important for your computer's overall health; proper security helps prevent viruses and malware, which allows programs to run quicker and smoother.

Filipino: Mahalaga ang computer security para maprotektahan ang impormasyon at mapanatiling maayos ang computer.

👀 Be aware of:

  • what you're downloading
  • what websites you visit
  • where emails come from
  • what links you click
  • what information you enter

🛡️ Ways to protect your computer

🔥 Use a firewallIt helps prevent unauthorized access.
🔄 Keep software updatedUse automatic updates when available.
🛡️ Use antivirusKeep antivirus software installed and current.
🔐 Protect passwordsChoose and protect passwords carefully.
🚫 Avoid suspicious linksDo not open unusual links or attachments.
🌐 Browse safelyAvoid unsafe or illicit sites and untrusted downloads.
💾 Be careful with USBsUse external devices you own or from a reliable source.
🧹 Care for hardwareKeep components dust-free and use proper cleaning methods.
