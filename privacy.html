Your Privacy Matters

Welcome to TEACHER ED Learning Hub. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use this website.

TEACHER ED Learning Hub is an educational website created to provide learning materials, lessons, activities, and other educational resources.

By using this website, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

TEACHER ED Learning Hub does not currently require visitors to create an account or provide personal information simply to browse the educational content.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, student records, or private academic information through the website.

2. How Information Is Used

If information is voluntarily provided through a future website feature, it may be used only for the purpose for which it was provided.

We aim to use information responsibly and only when reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, or improve the website.

3. Local Storage and Website Preferences

This website may use browser local storage to remember certain website preferences, such as your selected light or dark theme.

Local storage is stored by your browser on your device and is not intended to function as a personal student database.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies in the future when third-party services are added.

These technologies may be used to support website functionality, understand website usage, or display relevant advertising.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

TEACHER ED Learning Hub may use Google AdSense or other advertising services to help support the operation and development of the website.

Third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a user's visits to this and other websites.

Advertising practices may change as the website develops and additional services are introduced.

6. Third-Party Services and Links

The website may contain links to third-party websites, services, or educational resources.

These third-party websites have their own privacy policies and practices. TEACHER ED Learning Hub is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

7. Children's Privacy

TEACHER ED Learning Hub provides educational content that may be useful to students and younger learners.

The website does not intentionally request sensitive personal information from children through its basic educational content.

Parents, guardians, teachers, and learners should avoid submitting unnecessary personal information through online services.

8. Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information associated with the operation of the website.

However, no method of transmitting information over the Internet or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services, advertising arrangements, or legal requirements change.

Updates will be reflected on this page.

10. Contact
